# FEATHERFLOW — Flutter Route & Screen Audit

Routes are extracted statically from `lib/core/router/app_router.dart` and `lib/features/paper_portal/paper_portal_router.dart` (GoRouter, **hash URL strategy**: `http://127.0.0.1:8080/#/<route>`); 113 route entries, 125 `*Screen/*Page` widgets. **Static columns** (Loading/Empty/Error/Retry/Back/Permission-denied/Responsive) are regex heuristics over the screen source — they show the *presence of code*, not rendered behaviour. **Live columns** come from the Playwright crawl of 24 role sessions (routes reached, API calls seen, 320/390 px landing-screen screenshots) and from the direct deep-link tests (`guard_results.json`, `dbgroute`).

## 1. Findings

| # | Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | **No role-based route guard** — `_redirect` only checks authenticated/not; any signed-in user can open any shell (F-007). | live: farmer opened `#/admin`, `#/admin/users`, `#/admin/audit`, `#/pharmacy`, `#/doctor`, `#/delivery`, `#/research`, `#/feed-owner`; pharmacy owner opened `#/doctor`; support agent opened `#/admin/finance|team|pharmacy` |
